  • Clamp arms

  • Magnetic coupler
    3-stage scissor lift, floating head, N42SH ring magnets, 4 pogo pins, hall array
  • Sensors
    aft 100 mm IR tag, facade 300 mm tag, UWB anchors, side ToF, bumper switch
  • Airlock
    two bi-parting pressure doors on belt drives, EPDM seals, interlock (never both open), N₂ purge arches, dust grate + vacuum pump, equalisation valve

    zenith-facing, bimetal louvers, ammonia heat pipes
  • MLI warm box
    buffer battery, charger, MCU; survival heater at −20 °C
