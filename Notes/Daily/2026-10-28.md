# 2026-10-28

## Cargo loading / unloading: precise parking, smooth arm, physical pick and place

All in `Hunch/nasa-llaso-cad/js/lunar_logistics.js` (plus one line in `viewer.html`).

**Parking at the lander belt and the airlock table**
- Old gates let the arm start with the rover up to 7 cm / 10° (pickup) or 4.5 cm / 16° (drop-off) off its park pose; the arm then compensated, and a 6.7 cm short stop was measured.
- New fine-dock inside 0.30 m: creep down the park axis on a decelerating profile, stop inside 5 mm, pivot in place to within 0.46°, back out and re-run the axis if sideways error is over 1 cm (`PARK_TOL_*`, `FINE_ZONE`).
- Measured: from 25 cm / 8° off at the lander → 2.6 mm along, 3.7 mm side, 0.09° in 11 s. From 20 cm / 6° off at the depot → 4.6 mm, 3.0 mm, 0.13°.
- `viewer.html`: the 0.018 rad/s turn deadband now only zeroes drift when no turn is commanded. Before, it wiped every slow deliberate turn, so the rover could never correct a 1-2 cm offset (limit cycle backing out and back in). Hangar docking re-checked: 0 retries, 1.6 mm / 0.18° residual.

**Arm motion**
- Waypoints marked `via` merge into one monotone cubic (Fritsch-Carlson, no joint reversals between knots) timed by one quintic, so lift → transfer → hover flows without stops. Peak joint rate 1.05 rad/s carrying, 1.5 empty.
- Every segment starts from the arm's actual joints (`rebaseSeg`); grip-only steps hold the actual pose. Set-down segments re-solve IK live against the measured tip (`live`), which removes the 8.6 mm load-droop error at the far bay slot.
- Measured pod motion during pick: peak 2.3 m/s² and 0.38 m/s (was 28 m/s² and 1.25 m/s, with a 190° swing in 1.6 s and spline overshoot).

**Pick and place physics**
- Grasp: the bail is a 2-axis gimbal. The pod hangs plumb from the fingertip, turns with the base yaw and swings as a damped pendulum driven by the tip's acceleration (2 Hz, ζ 0.32, ±0.12 rad end stops). Any grasp offset is pulled out over 0.22 s (no snap).
- Root cause of the old 6.7 cm / 2.5 cm release error: the pod was rigid to the gripper and tilted 34° with the forearm (no wrist joint); the old release hid it with a 0.5-1 s sideways glide.
- Release: free fall under lunar g, bounce (restitution 0.18), guide-chamfer centring only while in contact, rocking flat; seats at the nearest half-turn orientation instead of spinning 180°.
- Measured: release within 0.5 mm (bay) and 0.7 mm (table) of centre, 4-5 mm drop as designed, no acceleration spikes over a full round trip.

Checks: all `tools/*_test.js` pass, `cad_model_check.js` 21/21, dock controller 100/100.

**Fixes in `viewer.html`**
- Rim deadlock: `computeAutopilot` scaled speed to 0 within 0.25 m of a rim whatever the heading, and stopped dead inside a bowl. Now: speed is cut only while closing on the rim; inside 0.45 m (or in a bowl) an escape mode pivots and drives out along the rim normal, leaning toward the goal (`nearestPitNormal`).
- `motionStepBlocked` zeroed any step with a bowl 0.5 m to the side, so a rover at a rim could never leave; a step that increases rim clearance is now always allowed.
- Crater-field routes still drive ~1.7x straight-line: checked, that is the planner going round an 8 m crater next to the goal, not waste.

**Wheel physics**: per-wheel spin from skid-steer kinematics (contact speed = v + yawRate·z; pivots counter-rotate), wheelspin when traction-limited, gearmotor/wheel inertia; suspension travel is a 9 Hz, ζ 0.55 spring-damper with bump stops instead of an exponential ease.

**Rover look (`js/fusion_rover.js`)**: palette cut to white / graphite / aluminium + one blue accent (red and amber kept only for E-stop, tail lights, SoC LEDs). Removed side panel-gap lines and red tabs, orange/red pod guide caps, brass knobs and bolt heads; the charge pad now sits on one central pedestal with a flared collar instead of a four-legged table. Pad height unchanged (coupler checks 21/21).

## Rover v4: swerve drive in a 24 in cube, canisters, physics, regolith

Design note: [[rover-v4-design]] (`Hunch/nasa-llaso-cad/review/docs/rover-v4-design.md`).

- **Rover** (`js/fusion_rover.js` v4):
  - 6 swerve modules (in-hub drive gearmotor, continuous steering servo + AS5600 + slip ring, coil-over); 12 motors.
  - Belly tub with the 5 Ah pack centred and low. The arm stows back over the deck.
  - Fits the 24 in cube stowed and with every module steered (`cad_model_check.js` 28/28).
- **BOM** (`js/rover_bom.js`): researched masses (pack 0.8 kg, servo 62 g, X2L 0.19 kg). 6.80 kg empty; CoM +31 mm empty, −35 mm loaded, 169 mm high.
- **Cargo:** domed pressure-vessel canisters with a grapple knob and trunnions, carried crosswise in V-saddles with latch hooks. `cargo_types` pod is now the canister (test updated).
- **Physics** (`viewer.html`), each verified:
  - swerve kinematics and steering rate;
  - per-wheel grip with traction control;
  - gravity feed-forward speed control, which fixed a stall on steep rims caused by the lower top speed;
  - sprung load transfer; active battery ballast;
  - rigid-body tip-over: holds at 2.0 m/s², rolls at 2.2, statics predict 2.12.
- **Regolith** (`js/terrain.js`, `viewer.html`):
  - The blocky ground was the shadow map: 2048² over 130 m = 6 cm texels.
  - Now a 4096² frustum of ±16 m follows the rover (~8 mm texels).
  - A 2048² normal map from the regolith height field replaces the aliasing bump map, with maximum anisotropy.
- **Dock fit:** park −1.538, pad offset −0.19, clamp jaws ±0.31. Simulator and website dock both latch first time. Dock images regenerated.
- **Checks:** all `tools/*_test.js` pass; dock controller 100/100; nav benchmark 12/12, 0 crashes, bowls, contacts or tip-overs; cargo round trip seats within 0.9 mm.

## Simulator render quality pass (`viewer.html`)

- **Image-based lighting:** `scene.environment` was off, so the metal and clear-coat panels reflected nothing. Added a PMREM environment (black sky, small intense sun disc, warm regolith bounce, faint earthshine); the matte regolith keeps `envMapIntensity` 0.12.
- **Exposure and albedo:** exposure 1.25 → 0.92 (it clipped the white paint and flattened the ground); regolith colour darkened toward real albedo for light/shadow contrast; ambient light 0.72 → 0.5.
- **Contact ambient occlusion:** soft patch under the body and between the wheels, a child of `roverGroup` so it tilts with the terrain.
- **Tiling:** the 48x repeat of the soil texture showed as rippled bands at distance. The terrain shader now multiplies albedo by two large-scale samples (rotated, different scales) and the normal strength is 0.85.
- **Chase camera** moved in (−2.7, 1.45, 0.35 m) to suit the 0.6 m rover.
- Not changed: default HUD panels (they cover much of the 3D view; `Hide panels` removes them).
