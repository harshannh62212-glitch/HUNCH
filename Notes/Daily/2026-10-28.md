# 2026-10-28

## Cargo loading / unloading: precise parking, smooth arm, physical pick and place

All in `Hunch/nasa-llaso-cad/js/lunar_logistics.js` (plus one line in `viewer.html`).

**Parking at the lander belt and the airlock table**
- Old gates let the arm start with the rover up to 7 cm / 10° (pickup) or 4.5 cm / 16° (drop-off) off its park pose; the arm then compensated, and a 6.7 cm short stop was measured.
- New fine-dock inside 0.30 m: creep down the park axis on a decelerating profile, stop inside 5 mm, pivot in place to within 0.46°, back out and re-run the axis if sideways error is over 1 cm (`PARK_TOL_*`, `FINE_ZONE`).
- Measured: from 25 cm / 8° off at the lander → 2.6 mm along, 3.7 mm side, 0.09° in 11 s. From 20 cm / 6° off at the depot → 4.6 mm, 3.0 mm, 0.13°.
- `viewer.html`: the 0.018 rad/s turn deadband now only zeroes drift when no turn is commanded. Before, it wiped every slow deliberate turn, so the rover could never correct a 1-2 cm offset (limit cycle backing out and back in). Hangar docking re-checked: 0 retries, 1.6 mm / 0.18° residual.

**Arm motion**
- Waypoints marked `via` merge into one monotone cubic (Fritsch-Carlson, no joint reversals between knots) timed by one quintic, so lift → transfer → hover flows without stops. Peak joint rate 1.05 rad/s carrying, 1.5 empty.
- Every segment starts from the arm's actual joints (`rebaseSeg`); grip-only steps hold the actual pose. Set-down segments re-solve IK live against the measured tip (`live`), which removes the 8.6 mm load-droop error at the far bay slot.
- Measured pod motion during pick: peak 2.3 m/s² and 0.38 m/s (was 28 m/s² and 1.25 m/s, with a 190° swing in 1.6 s and spline overshoot).

**Pick and place physics**
- Grasp: the bail is a 2-axis gimbal. The pod hangs plumb from the fingertip, turns with the base yaw and swings as a damped pendulum driven by the tip's acceleration (2 Hz, ζ 0.32, ±0.12 rad end stops). Any grasp offset is pulled out over 0.22 s (no snap).
- Root cause of the old 6.7 cm / 2.5 cm release error: the pod was rigid to the gripper and tilted 34° with the forearm (no wrist joint); the old release hid it with a 0.5-1 s sideways glide.
- Release: free fall under lunar g, bounce (restitution 0.18), guide-chamfer centring only while in contact, rocking flat; seats at the nearest half-turn orientation instead of spinning 180°.
- Measured: release within 0.5 mm (bay) and 0.7 mm (table) of centre, 4-5 mm drop as designed, no acceleration spikes over a full round trip.
