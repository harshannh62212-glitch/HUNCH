# 2026-10-27

## Dock station page: grey redesign with an airlock

- `Hunch/nasa-llaso-cad/dock-station-cad.html` now models the dock as a grey panelled garage with an **airlock** in place of the sectional door.
  - Outer pressure door at x 2.15 (the old door plane), inner pressure door at x 0.10, vestibule between (rover stops at x 1.12, ~0.3 m clear fore and aft).
  - Doors are bi-parting leaves on belt drives with EPDM seals and portholes. **Interlock:** a door only opens when the other is shut and the vestibule pressure matches its side (0 kPa outside, 10 kPa N₂ in the bay).
  - Entry: outer opens → rover into vestibule → outer seals → N₂ purge (2.6 s) → pressurise → inner opens → existing capture/latch. Exit is the reverse, with a vent step.
  - Vestibule fit-out: dust grate over a vacuum plenum, two purge arches with nozzles, N₂ tank, vacuum pump, equalisation valve and gauge, roof vent stack, status lamps (red/amber/green).
  - Cutaway now sections both bulkheads on the centreline (clipping plane) and ghosts the roof, PV array and near wall.
- Only the CAD page changed. `js/dock_station.js` and the simulator (`viewer.html`) still use the sectional door.
- Website: regenerated `assets/screens/dock-overview.jpg`, `dock-coupler.jpg`, `dock-door.jpg` (now the airlock purge shot) and bumped their `?v=` cache tags.
- Checks: `node tools/cad_model_check.js` 21/21, `node tools/dock_controller_test.js` 100/100, full depart → return cycle in the page with the doors never open together.
- Deploys: the live site only changes after `scripts/deploy_site.sh`; a git push alone does not update ageisv1.vercel.app.
