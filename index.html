Autonomous Surface Logistics for the Lunar Base Camp

AEGIS-V1 (Autonomous External Ground Logistics & Transfer System) is designed to solve the critical last-mile challenge on the Moon: autonomously offloading, securing, and transporting standardized payload containers from landing vehicles directly to lunar habitat facilities across extreme south-pole terrain.
